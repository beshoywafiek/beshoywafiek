# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who can hire a freelance graphic designer, confirmed by the owner as all
of these (2026-10-06):

- Small and medium business owners in Egypt — restaurants, clinics, real estate,
  sports academies — where the owner decides and usually messages on WhatsApp.
- Brands in the Gulf, with higher budgets and higher expectations.
- Agencies with overflow work looking for a designer they can rely on.
- Clients abroad (US / Europe) paying in dollars and judging reliability.

Most arrive on a phone, from Behance, Instagram or a shared link. Their job:
decide in a few minutes whether this designer can make their brand look
serious, and what it costs.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio and sales site of Beshoy Wafiek, a graphic designer in
Cairo designing since 2021. It shows every published project in full and sells
three services. Success is a qualified enquiry: a WhatsApp message or a filled
lead form.

## Positioning

**Social media design is the service to lead with** (owner, 2026-10-06): it is
the bulk of the work (251 of 458 published pieces) and is sold monthly, from
$80+. Brand identity ($300+, from 10 days) and logo design ($180+, from 5 days)
follow. What a template shop cannot claim: one designer who builds the identity
and then the social system on top of it, fluently in Arabic and English, with
every published piece on show rather than a curated handful.

## Operating Context

- Visitors compare on a phone, often mid-conversation on WhatsApp.
- Prices are starting prices; every project is quoted to its scope.
- He replies "usually within 2 hours".
- Contact: **both WhatsApp and the lead form**, side by side, the visitor picks
  (owner, 2026-10-06). The form falls back to WhatsApp until `FORM_ENDPOINT` is
  set (`_source/content.py`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages, generated by `_source/build.py`; no framework,
  no build step at runtime. All rules in `CLAUDE.md` are binding.
- Bilingual on one URL, Arabic-first (RTL), Egyptian colloquial Arabic.
- Project images are hot-linked from Behance's CDN (owner has not downloaded
  them yet); each piece has a known size and aspect ratio in `js/projects.json`.
- Services: Logo Design, Brand Identity, Social Media. Web design was offered
  once and removed; not currently sold.
- No custom domain yet; the owner will buy one once the look is settled.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Beshoy Wafiek / بيشوي وفيق. Logo and favicon in `images/`.
- Identity fixed in the stylesheet header: #0B0A0A, #F4F2EF, #E85002, #C10801;
  Cairo and Space Mono. (Recorded as the incumbent; a redesign may revisit it
  with the owner.)
- Voice: first person, plain, confident, Egyptian colloquial in Arabic.

## Evidence on Hand

- 25 published projects, 458 pieces, real client names (`_source/meta.py`),
  each with a written challenge / approach / delivered story.
- Behance figures supplied by the owner: 10 agencies, 290 appreciations,
  3,688+ views (`STATS` in `content.py`).
- Profile photo `images/profile.jpg`.
- **No client testimonials.** `PROOF` stays empty until he supplies real ones.
  No project outcome metrics exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the argument: show real pieces at their real proportions, fast,
   before asking for anything.
2. Lead with social media, and show it as a system — a grid that holds — not as
   loose posts.
3. Every claim is checkable: counts derived from the work, prices stated, no
   invented proof.
4. The phone is the primary screen; Arabic is the primary language.
5. One step to contact from anywhere, by WhatsApp or the form.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full RTL and LTR parity; reduced-motion respected everywhere; tap targets 44px
on touch; text contrast 4.5:1 (enforced by the gates in `_source/tests/`).
